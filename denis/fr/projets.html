Jade River overhaul

DEME3 engines · 7,460 kW

DEME is a Belgian dredging company with a large fleet of dredgers. A dredger is experiencing a lot of varying loads on their engines, making the parts dispense huge. Reliability is a must for them.

Lire le compte rendu complet

The Jade River is a Trailing Suction Hopper Dredger. This is a ship with very powerful pumps and motors, that can suck sand, clay, silt and even gravel out of the bottom of the water. The Jade River is equipped with two boating-engines and a pump-engine. The total power of the DRACULA system is 7,460 kW.

During the period from January 7th until the trial on February 10th, we completed a full revision of the 3 engines. Time management was a priority for us, as the ship went right back in business.

With a team of 10, and at peak times even 15 dedicated employees, we started with the disassembly of the three engines.

Through a customized transport the parts went to our workshop in Schoten.

There was a team ready to get the parts cleaned, machined, checked and assembled. Again with the necessary expertise.

For such a revision, we work out an action plan, so we always proceed as efficiently as possible and transports can be aligned to this. Starting from the disassembly, there was a team present in the workplace to overhaul the components. The Cylinder heads were fully inspected, cleaned and corroded. Liners were cleansed, shimmed, honed and undergone a crack test. Valves were grinded and injectors pressure tested. Pistons were checked and piston rings were renewed. Also oil and fuel pumps were completely overhauled.

Meanwhile, our team was able to continue work on board. All bearings were replaced (head-, connecting rod- and camshaft bearings). There were different machining operations completed on board, including the boring of the landing surfaces of the liners. The crankshaft was polished and clearances were taken. Meanwhile, all vibration dampers were dismantled and overhauled. We made sure that the components off board were on time at our workshop, whenever the other components were ready at the workshop. We always looked for the fastest possible action. This we achieved, by identifying our components and sorting them out.

At each delivery we took a check-up. This meant that all the parts from the workshop also arrived back on board and vice versa.

Despite some additional tasks, such as replacing the bourages of all generator engines, we were able to finish the work on time and with a perfect supply of the required spare-parts.

Lange Wapper overhaul

DEME2× MAN 32/40

In the port of Antwerp we overhauled two engines of a ‘trailer suction hopper dredger’ from DEME, the “Lange Wapper”. It concerned a top overhaul of two MAN 32/40 engines (bore 320mm). This means that the parts of the cylinder heads are checked and replaced if necessary. In this case we had possession of spare cylinder heads, which ensured that the ship was back in business very quickly. Some additional works like checking the vibration damper of the camshaft and the exchange of a fuel pipe were carried out at the same time. Also some pistons were disassembled to check the bearings for possible damage.

Al-Idrisi overhaul

Jan De Nul2× MAN 8L32/40

Al-Idrisi is a brand new Trailer Suction Hopper Dredger from the Jan De Nul-fleet (built in 2012). A standard overhaul of the main engines was designated after 12000 operating hours.

Lire le compte rendu complet

There are 2 MAN boating-engines (type 8L32/40), each one provides 4000kW power.

The overhaul of main engines was carried out on board. The cylinder heads, pistons, con rods,fire rings and liners were dismantled and send to our workshop for overhaul. All parts were chemically cleaned. A Full load test program according to MAN instructions was performed after assembling.

16x 32/40 cyl.heads overhaul

  • Dismantling,chemical cleaning and calibration of the components
  • Hydraulic test 7bar is performed
  • O-rings of the injector sleeves are renewed
  • 15 valves were replaced
  • All exhaust guides were renewed, inlet guides still in tolerance
  • Valves were grinded to correct angle
  • Valve seats and valves are grinded with Hunger equipment
  • The safety valves were overhauled, adjusted and calibrated to the correct opening pressure
  • The starting air valves are overhauled,the seats are lapped and new PTFE seals are installed
  • All components are installed with new o-rings and gaskets
  • The cylinder heads were assembled and returned to the ship, covered in plastic and anti-corrosion protected

23x Fuel injectors

  • All injector nozzles are renewed
  • Set of spare-injectors provided
  • The injector bodies are grinded/lapped on the high pressure sealing surfaces
  • The injectors are tested and calibrated to correct opening pressure

16x Safety valves

  • Dismantling of all components
  • Chemical cleaning and examination of parts
  • Grinding an lapping of the needles and the related seats
  • Reassembling and adjusting on test bench at 247 bar

16x liners

  • Chemical cleaning of the liners is performed
  • Both landing faces are cleaned,and where subject to dye-check crack test
  • Before and after honing a calibration report was made
  • All liners are returned to the ship with anti-corrosion protection layer

16x fire land ring

  • Chemical cleaning and sand blasting is performed on all rings
  • All are subjected to dye-check crack test, no cracks were found
  • Lapping of both landing faces was performed with dummy lapping tools
  • Removal of the blasting disposal and cleaning again is performed
  • The landing faces were protected with tectyl and the fire rings are returned to the ship

16x pistons

  • Dismantling of the connecting rod (small-end and piston-pin)
  • Chemical cleaning of the components
  • Calibration of the piston pin, the corresponding small end bush and piston pin holes
  • Calibration of the piston ring grooves
  • Assembling of the pistons with the connecting rods

Inspection of main bearings

Overhaul of 2x Turbo’s NR34S

  • Both cartridges dismantled
  • One spare cartridge installed
  • Both dismantled cartridges are overhauled and returned to vessel /warehouse DN

Sanderus overhaul

Jan De NulSMIT-MAN · 875 kW

Sanderus is one of the oldest ships of Jan De Nul's fleet.

Lire le compte rendu complet

The ship was built in 1967 and has a 'hopper capacity' of 5300m³.

There's 6780kW installed dieselpower, from which the dredge pump engine that we overhauled contains 875kW.

This engine is an old SMIT-MAN.

In association with a local partner, we ensured that the ship was back in business after 3 weeks.

All components like cylinder heads, pistons, liners,...were disassembled and transported to the workshop.

Meanwhile our men continued in the engineroom with measuring, checking of the crankshaft and camshaft etc. If necessary bearings were replaced.

In the workshop all components were checked for cracks or irregularities. All surfaces were grinded or lapped. The cylinder liners were honed. Afterwards all components were measured again and packed for transport.
