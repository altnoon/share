Our products and services

We deliver spare parts, overhauls and complete turbocharger solutions — from small components to full assemblies.

What we offer:

  • Genuine and OEM-compatible parts from global partners
  • Complete overhaul sets and main spares (rotors, casings, nozzle and cover rings, etc.)
  • Professional service both on board and in our Antwerp workshop

Our overhaul process includes:

  1. 01

    Disassembly and detailed inspection

  2. 02

    Cleaning, blasting, and rotor balancing

  3. 03

    Bearing casing cleaning and painting

  4. 04

    Cartridge or full turbocharger assembly

  5. 05

    Comprehensive service report upon completion

Which spare parts do we offer?

A consistent supply of turbocharger spare parts is essential for maintaining reliable engine performance across different applications. Our inventory includes key components such as rotors, bearings, seal rings, casings and nozzle assemblies, selected to match the specific requirements of your installation. Each item undergoes strict quality control to ensure material durability and operational compatibility. By using our turbocharger services, operators strengthen long-term engine health and limit unplanned downtime during demanding service conditions.

  • Bearings
  • Seal rings
  • Casings
  • Nozzle assemblies
