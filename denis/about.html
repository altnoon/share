Denis Engine Services & Parts, that’s a team!

Our inbound team finds you the right spare parts, on time and at the right price. You send us your engine details, we deliver the spare parts you need. Fast and with the best possible technical service included.

Our experienced outbound teams go abroad to maintain or repair marine and industrial engines on site, spare parts included. The more complex the job, the more we like it.

We go way back.

Denis Engine started more than 20 years ago. The main focus was to maintain and repair medium speed diesel engines of Deutz and MAN on site. No place was too far. Our teams crossed the oceans back and forth to make engines run smooth and keep businesses up and running. The philosophy of ‘work efficient and think outside the box’ was already in place.

As days and years passed by, the technical expertise of our teams grew and the second generation took over.

Today, we service, overhaul and maintain all major engine brands among which ABC, Caterpillar, Deutz and MAN. Of course, with the philosophy of efficient work and thinking outside the box in heart and mind.

Because one thing never changed: all our people are passionate about their profession and engines in general. We love to fix what’s broken and are thrilled by the sound of a smooth running engine.

Our team is ready to keep your business up and running. Are you too?
