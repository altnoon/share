Provenance & caveats — how the charts were built

MitigationIM effort, conservative lines, walk anything you would not ride at home. Two wet-surface sessions before October

Navigation → DQFollow the track, never the rider ahead.

Why it's realGravel leg unsigned — GPS or nothing; 2024 riders got lost following each other

MitigationGPX on head unit + phone. Follow the track, never the rider ahead. Trail leg is marked

Mechanical in Cap de CreusTwo tubes, boot, pump — one rehearsed change.

Why it's realRocky, technical, before the first aid at km 34.2 — the one sector where a mechanical costs the race

MitigationTubes, so nothing self-seals — 2 correct tubes, boot, pump, one rehearsed change. Hold 2.2/2.4 bar, pick lines. Buffer absorbs one flat, not three

MitigationDraft on the plain — it is legal and it is free. Sleeves on, bag down the front if cold-dry
