Provenance & caveats — how the charts were built

How to read it. Terrain and distance are real, from the organiser's route. Feed positions come from your bike time with the water stops at about 42% (W1) and 76% (W2) of it — the technical first third is slower per km — so the clock time is the instruction and the km is the illustration. The run uses its own km scale.

Held facts: elevation remains disputed: the race briefing says 900 m, the course page 1,200 m; the organiser's own route data measures 1,017 m, so plan for ~1,000. Profile drawn from the 2025-edition route; the official 2026 GPX supersedes it. Wetsuits are expected — the briefing tells you to put yours on in the changing tents.

Rules that carry penalties

  • DQ — course deviation or missed control point. The gravel leg is unsigned: GPS only. The trail leg is marked, red ribbons and blue arrows. Follow the track, not the rider ahead.
  • +30 min — taking supplies outside an aid zone. Carry all fuel; anything taken at a station is legal — water refills at 34.2 / 68 are mandatory, course Maurten = emergency fallback.
  • +30 min — riding the bike inside T1.
